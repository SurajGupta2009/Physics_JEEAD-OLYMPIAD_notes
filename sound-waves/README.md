## Prerequisite graph

```
String Waves (transverse, v=√(T/μ), standing waves)
      ↓
Sound Waves (longitudinal, v=√(B/ρ), pressure vs displacement) ← you are here
      ↓
EM Waves (transverse EM, Poynting)
      ↓
Wave Optics (interference uses same superposition)
```

- **Requires:** SHM, Newton's second law, bulk modulus, ideal gas $PV=RT$, logarithms.
- **Leads to:** Doppler in optics (relativistic), shock waves in fluids, acoustic resonators.

## Teaching contract

- Every boxed formula carries condition of validity (e.g., $|\partial s/\partial x|≪1$, $r≪λ$, $v_s<v$, adiabatic).
- No hand-waving: $v$ derived from $F=ma$ on gas slab, Laplace from $PV^γ=$ const, intensity from energy density, standing waves from boundary conditions, Doppler from $λ'$ vs $v_{\text{rel}}$ distinction.
- Diagrams over prose where geometry does work – 15 local SVGs, no external links.
- Questions interleaved with collapsible `<details>` solutions, each with check.
