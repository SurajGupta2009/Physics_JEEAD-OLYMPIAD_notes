THERMODYNAMICS — PRINTING

Prefer thermodynamics-short-notes.pdf: exact A4 portrait pages, JEE first.
Select A4 portrait, ONE sheet per page, Fit to page / Fit printable area.
Do NOT use Fill, Crop, poster tiling, multiple-up or borderless expansion.
For PNG printing: 2480 x 3508 px, embedded 300 DPI; same settings.
White safe inset is at least 125 px (about 10.6 mm).
Main prose 11.52 pt; explanations 11.04 pt; equations typeset separately.
PDFs retain all source pixels losslessly; bookmarks follow reading order.
Test one page before a full run. Colour is preferable; grayscale remains
readable, but check yellow result panels against your printer settings.
Read Part I (21 JEE sheets), then Part II (7 specialised extensions).
Independent numbering is intentional. Combined ZIP is PNGs only, with
JEE files inserted first. Extract both directories; do not flatten them.
