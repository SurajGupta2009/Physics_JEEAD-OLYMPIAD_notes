# Scope and classification — sound waves short notes

The split between Part I (JEE Main + Advanced) and Part II (Olympiad / extensions)
follows **syllabus relevance and usefulness, not difficulty**. No topic is claimed to
be "impossible in JEE": items move to Part II when they are specialised techniques
rather than core exam tools.

## Part I — JEE Main + Advanced (60 sheets)

Core chapter topics, in teaching order (sheet titles vary slightly by packing):

1. Longitudinal mechanical waves; s(x,t); λ = v/f; wave speed vs particle speed
2. Strain and the acoustic pressure; ΔP = −B ∂s/∂x; ΔP₀ = ρvωs₀
3. Displacement–pressure duality; quadrature; energy density ½ρω²s₀²
4. The wave equation and v = √(B/ρ); non-dispersive character
5. Waves in solids: rods √(Y/ρ); bulk √((K+4G/3)/ρ)
6. Newton's isothermal estimate (280 m/s) and the time-scale argument
7. Laplace's adiabatic correction: v = √(γRT/M)
8. The temperature law v = 331.3√(T/273) in numbers
9. Molar mass, humidity, and pressure-independence
10. Intensity: I = ΔP₀²/2ρv = ½ρvω²s₀²
11. Point and line sources: 1/r² vs 1/r spreading
12. Decibel scale; 10 vs 20 log rules; level addition
13. Superposition and interference; coherent vs incoherent
14. Quincke's tube; the 2ΔL path rule
15. Beats: f_b = |f₁ − f₂|; the wax test; envelopes
16. Standing waves; nodes/antinodes; boundary conditions
17. Closed pipes: odd harmonics (2n−1)v/4L; overtone naming
18. Open pipes: all harmonics nv/2L; spectra identification
19. End correction e ≈ 0.6r (0.85r flanged); L_eff rules
20. Resonance tube: v = 2f(l₂ − l₁), e = (l₂ − 3l₁)/2
21. Kundt's tube: dust heaps at λ/2; v_gas = v_rod d/L_rod
22. Doppler: moving source
23. Doppler: moving observer; the second-order asymmetry
24. Conventions and the combined formula; reverse estimation
25. Wind: v_eff = v + w; no shift for fixed parties; round-trip cancellation
26. 2D Doppler; closest approach; glide slope f u²/(v b)
27. Echo and double Doppler f(v+u)/(v−u); beats with echo
28. Mach cone sin α = 1/M; sonic boom; delay geometry

## Part II — Olympiad / specialised extensions (24 sheets)

1. Acoustic impedance Z = ρv; coupling and matching
2. Reflection and transmission: R = ((Z₂−Z₁)/(Z₂+Z₁))², T = 4Z₁Z₂/(Z₁+Z₂)²
3. Pipes as impedance problems: input impedance, Q, resonance ladders
4. Phasor superposition; array factor sin(Nψ/2)/sin(ψ/2)
5. Energy flux, radiation resistance, Q, ring-down
6. Sign-free vector Doppler f′ = f(c − v_o·r̂)/(c − v_s·r̂)
7. Accelerated sources; retarded time; the glide curve
8. Ranging from frequency slope; echo beats; two-beam tracking
9. WKB ducts and horns: I·A = const; amplitude ∝ A^(−1/2)
10. Three elastic speeds; seismology patterns
11. Mach geometry clinic (delay, emission point, carpet)
12. Acoustic estimation kit and Fermi drills

## Notes on packing

Cards are packed to near capacity and never shrink body text; long teacher
expositions are split across continuation cards, so a topic may span several
sheets and a sheet may carry the tail of the previous topic. Card titles keep the
topic identity; continuation cards are marked "(cont.)".

## What is deliberately NOT here

- Full treatment of every worked exercise of the source chapter (the representative
  ones are used as checks and examples)
- Nonlinear acoustics, shock structure details, and numerical methods
- Musical acoustics beyond what illustrates the physics (formants, pipe timbre)
- Relativistic Doppler (only the comparison remark in Oly-06)
