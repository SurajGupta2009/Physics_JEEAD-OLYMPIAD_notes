# Printing — sound waves short notes

- Paper: **A4 portrait**, one sheet per page.
- Scaling: **Fit to page** — never Fill, never Crop. The sheets are authored at
  exact A4 proportions (2480×3508 px at 300 DPI), so "Fit" is 100% and loses nothing;
  "Fill/Crop" would clip the margins and the footer.
- Colour: print in colour (teal/yellow/red encode meaning). If forced to grayscale,
  the yellow equation chips stay legible; the teal/red distinction degrades but the
  labels "WHY /", "CHECK /", "WATCH /" carry it.
- Duplex: flip on the long edge. The sheets are designed to survive duplex printing
  (no bleed-dependent content).
- PDFs already have one sheet per exact A4 page with bookmarks; print the PDF for
  predictable results. `sound-all-pngs.zip` is for screen use and re-layout.
- Browser printing of `index.html` is also A4-safe: the print stylesheet emits one
  210×297 mm sheet per page.
