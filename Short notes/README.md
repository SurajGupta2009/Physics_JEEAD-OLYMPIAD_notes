# Physics short notes

All generated notes follow one layout:

```text
Short notes/
├── Capacitors/
│   ├── JEE-01-foundations.png
│   ├── … other JEE- and Olympiad- PNG sheets
│   ├── capacitors-short-notes.pdf
│   ├── … part PDFs and ZIP bundles
│   └── index.html
├── Thermodynamics/
│   ├── JEE-01-temperature.png
│   ├── … 21 JEE and 7 Olympiad sheets, 3 PDFs and 3 ZIPs
│   └── index.html
└── Rigid_Body_Dynamics/
    ├── JEE-01-kinematics.png
    ├── … other JEE- and Olympiad- PNG sheets
    ├── rigid-body-dynamics-short-notes.pdf
    ├── … part PDFs and ZIP bundles
    └── index.html
```

**PNGs and PDFs sit directly inside their chapter folder.** `JEE-` and `Olympiad-` filename prefixes retain the relevance-based distinction and independent numbering. Each chapter also keeps its gallery, coverage guide, editable source, export scripts and licensed font assets alongside its deliverables.
