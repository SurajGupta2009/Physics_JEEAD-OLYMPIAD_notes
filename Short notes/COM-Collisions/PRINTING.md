# Printing — COM & collisions

- **A4, portrait, one sheet per page.** Scaling: **Fit to page** / Fit / Shrink oversized pages. Do not use Fill or Crop.
- Sheets are authored at 2480×3508 pixels, 300 DPI, with about 10 mm safe margins. Actual-size printing on A4 also works.
- Combined PDF is JEE-first and bookmarked; separate JEE and Olympiad PDFs are available.
- Colour palette: navy body text, teal headings/WHY/CHECK, yellow results, red warnings. For a bound set, print `com-collisions-jee.pdf` followed by `com-collisions-olympiad.pdf`, or use `com-collisions-short-notes.pdf`.
