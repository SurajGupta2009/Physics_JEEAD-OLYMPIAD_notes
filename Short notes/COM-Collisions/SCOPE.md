# Scope — JEE core vs specialised extension

Boundary principle: syllabus relevance and usefulness, not difficulty. Hard core applications stay in JEE; no topic is described as something that can never appear in JEE.

## Part I — JEE Main + Advanced (49 sheets)

Discrete and continuous COMs; standard rods, rings, discs, hemispheres, cones, arcs and triangles; negative-mass hole trick; motion of the COM and explosions; impulse and average force; conservation of momentum; elastic/inelastic/sticking collisions and restitution; 1-D master formulas and special cases; reduced mass and energy loss; impulses of deformation and reformation (Cengage §1.22); oblique collisions and line-of-impact decomposition; equal-mass 90° theorem; ballistic pendulum and bullet/block stages; spring compression after collisions, including the 3M > m condition; CM-frame decomposition; sand/belts/hoppers; rocket equation; chain/jet momentum flux; man-on-boat and wedge; bounce heights and series; moving walls/platforms; examiner traps; triage, validity and results ledger. This covers the source chapter core and Cengage Mechanics II ch. 1 floor, including its COM, impulse, collision, variable-mass and rocket treatments.

## Part II — Olympiad / specialised extensions (26 sheets)

Falling-chain 3mg result · CM-frame scattering and deflection limits · impulse approximation · driven-platform bouncing · chain over a peg (ODE and contact loss) · two-stage rockets · water-jet flux and turbine power · folded chain · relativistic momentum preview · hopper cart · momentum integration methods. The source's OL1–OL10 topics are retained, with synthesis sheets for hopper and integration methods. Some applications can also be useful for JEE Advanced; placement in Part II reflects the longer treatment, not a claim of syllabus exclusion.

## Hand-offs / not covered in depth

- Spin, rough-sphere angular impulses and hinge-heavy collisions: rigid-body dynamics (source hand-off to PART 8).
- Full relativistic dynamics beyond the momentum preview: source PART 28.
- General fluid-mechanics momentum equation: source PART 11; only flux applications appear here.
- Exhaustive worked-exercise coverage: representative drills only.
