# Scope and concept-by-concept classification

Membership is relevance/usefulness, NOT difficulty or the source's difficulty labels. Part I retains demanding first-law/path/cycle work and thermal-mechanical combinations. Part II isolates specialised statistical/real-fluid/open-system tools. No claim is made that an extension can never appear in JEE: it may be derivable with supplied information. This is not an official current-syllabus or question-frequency audit.

## Part I — 21 JEE sheets

The core connects temperature/expansion/calorimetry to ideal-gas kinetics, energy ledgers, constrained paths, engines, entropy and heat transfer. Hard algebra and multi-stage constraints stay here. Thermal expansion, calorimetry, conduction/radiation and mechanical applications overlap other JEE chapters but are intentionally included as thermal-physics revision, not falsely presented as entirely exclusive to a narrow thermodynamics syllabus. Mean free path/effusion basics are useful adjacent kinetic tools; full flux-weighted beam cooling/escape is deferred. Otto/Diesel/Stirling are constrained ideal-gas applications with their construction explained, not a promise that engine names must be memorised. Entropy and finite bodies are retained as useful Advanced-level reasoning, while formal availability and detailed blowdown are separated.

## Part II — 7 specialised extension sheets

- **01 Microstates & quantum mode activation** — Microstate counting, indistinguishability and partition functions require additional statistical tools; ordinary active-mode equipartition stays in Part I.
- **02 Effusive beams & thermal escape** — Velocity-weighted beams, leaking-vessel cooling and qualified Jeans escape go beyond the introductory flux ratio.
- **03 Atmospheres: hydrostatics plus a model** — Hydrostatic atmospheres, dry/moist profiles and parcel stability combine gravity with specialised atmospheric assumptions; ordinary hydrostatics can still be JEE mechanics.
- **04 Real gases: interactions & criticality** — Real-fluid criticality, Maxwell construction and caloric equations need interactions and equilibrium machinery beyond the fixed ideal-gas model.
- **05 Throttling is not free expansion** — Steady-flow enthalpy, Joule-Thomson sign and inversion limits are specialised open-system/real-gas tools.
- **06 Photon gas & radiative applications** — Ordinary Stefan-Boltzmann/Wien laws remain in Part I. Photon-fluid entropy/adiabats and effective-planet/redshift applications are extensions.
- **07 Useful work & compressed-tank limits** — Formal environmental availability and enthalpy-outflow tank models are distinct from closed-system gas paths. Phase-onset and safety limitations are explicit.

## Retained and deliberately omitted

All sheets combine formulas and explanations. Final-results-only compression was rejected. The detailed source retains its full worked paper: these pages consolidate the mechanisms, derivations and important traps rather than repeat every exercise. No invented equilibrium path is drawn for a strongly irreversible jump. No engineering certification, precise real-air condensation pressure, universal loss timescale, exact moist lapse rate or exhaustive verification is claimed. Diagrams indicate their specific idealisations and scaled axes; atmospheric and material constants are representative values.
