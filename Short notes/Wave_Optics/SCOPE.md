## Guiding Boundary

Every concept, derivation, and worked-problem insight from [`../../wave-optics/Wave-optics.md`](../../wave-optics/Wave-optics.md) (`12` parts, `136` questions, `28` figures) is preserved and split strictly by **syllabus relevance** rather than difficulty:

- **Part I — JEE Main + Advanced (`JEE-01` .. `JEE-16`, 16 A4 sheets, 64 cards, 32 analytic diagrams):** Complete NCERT + JEE Main + JEE Advanced Wave Optics syllabus — wavefronts, Fermat & Huygens proofs, phasors, visibility, YDSE geometry in 2D/3D (hyperboloids, end-on circular fringes, tilted screen, full-circle spot counts, slabs, liquids, oblique incidence, moving sources, bichromatic & white light, channelled spectrum), thin parallel films, air wedges, Newton's rings, single-layer AR coatings, Fresnel biprism, Lloyd's mirror, Billet split lens, Fraunhofer single-slit & double-slit diffraction (missing orders, exact visible peak counts), basic transmission gratings, Airy disc & Rayleigh resolution (microscope NA, telescope, eye, optimal pinhole camera), polarisation (Malus's law, crossed polaroids with rotating middle sheet, Brewster reflection & internal Brewster identity $\sin\theta_c = \cot\theta_B$, Rayleigh $\lambda^{-4}$ scattering, birefringence, HWP/QWP, Biot's optical activity), and a rapid JEE master triage sheet.
- **Part II — Olympiad / Specialised Extensions (`Olympiad-01` .. `Olympiad-08`, 8 A4 sheets, 32 cards, 16 analytic diagrams):** INPhO / IPhO / APhO wave-optics machinery — Michelson interferometer (Haidinger vs Fizeau fringes, compensator plate, gas cell index), temporal & spatial coherence (sodium D-doublet beats, Wiener-Khinchin Fourier transform, van Cittert-Zernike theorem, Michelson stellar interferometer), exact Fresnel TE/TM amplitude & power laws, Stokes reciprocity ($r' = -r$, $r^2 + t t' = 1$), multilayer quarter-wave HR stacks, continuous TIR phase shifts, achromatic Fresnel rhomb, evanescent field & Frustrated TIR barrier tunnelling, Goos-Hanchen shift, exact $N$-slit Dirichlet kernel, Chromatic resolving power $\mathcal{R} = mN$, FSR, blazed echelle gratings, Fabry-Perot Airy function & laser cavities, Fresnel half-period zones, Babinet's principle & Arago-Poisson bright spot, Fresnel zone plates, Cornu spiral & knife-edge diffraction, Abbe Fourier spatial filtering & Zernike phase contrast, 3D X-ray Bragg reflection ($n = 1 - \delta < 1$), Sagnac fiber gyro (Fresnel drag cancellation), Hanbury Brown-Twiss intensity interferometry, and off-axis holography.
