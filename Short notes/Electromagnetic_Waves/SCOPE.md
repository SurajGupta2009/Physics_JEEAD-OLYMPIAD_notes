# Scope and Classification — Electromagnetic Waves

- **Part I (`JEE-01`–`JEE-12`, 12 sheets):** Covers the complete JEE Main + JEE Advanced curriculum plus foundational bridges to Wave Optics and Modern Physics — displacement current, gap $B_\phi(r)$, dielectric polarisation current, all four Maxwell laws in vacuum/matter, curl-curl derivations, $v = c/\sqrt{\mu_r\varepsilon_r}$, vector triad reconstruction, linear/circular/elliptical polarisation & Malus's law, Poynting theorem, peak vs RMS intensity, $1/r^2$ scaling, oblique flat-plate radiation pressure & shear force, full 7-band EM spectrum, and oscillating LC / dipole radiation.
- **Part II (`Olympiad-01`–`Olympiad-06`, 6 sheets):** Covers INPhO / IPhO & advanced JEE long-form extensions from §2.11, §4, and Q29–Q36 of `Electromagnetic-waves.md` — radial Poynting inflow into capacitors/wires & coax TEM modes, sphere/cone/Lambertian radiation integrals & stellar blow-out $\beta$-ratio, impedance boundary matching & $\lambda/4$ anti-reflection transformers, standing-wave node quadrature & cavity radiation $P = u/3$, Ohmic skin depth $\delta$ & plasma dispersion $v_p v_g = c^2$, and relativistic moving mirrors, Poynting–Robertson drag & parallel-plate $\mathrm{TE}_m$ waveguides.
