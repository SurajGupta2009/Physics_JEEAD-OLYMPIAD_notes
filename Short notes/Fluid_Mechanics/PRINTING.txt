FLUID MECHANICS — PRINTING

Page:        A4 portrait, 210 x 297 mm
Each sheet:  one page; do not print two pages per sheet
Scaling:     Fit / Fit to page  (NOT Fill, NOT Crop, NOT 100 % on a Letter printer)
Margins:     none / minimum — the sheet already carries a 9–10 mm safe margin
Colour:      required (navy prose, teal headings, yellow results, red warnings)
Paper:       plain 80–100 gsm is enough; 120 gsm if you write on them
Duplex:      long edge if you print the two parts as one booklet

PDF:         fluid-mechanics-short-notes.pdf  (30 pages, bookmarked)
             fluid-mechanics-jee.pdf           (19 pages, Part I)
             fluid-mechanics-olympiad.pdf      (11 pages, Part II)
Bookmarks:   one per sheet, "JEE 07: <question>" style, use them in the sidebar.

PNG:         print at 300 DPI, "actual size", no resampling. 2480 x 3508 px is
             exactly A4 at 300 DPI.

HTML:        open index.html, Ctrl+P; the print stylesheet already emits one
             sheet per page with no site chrome.

If text looks clipped at the edge, the printer is scaling up: switch to Fit.
