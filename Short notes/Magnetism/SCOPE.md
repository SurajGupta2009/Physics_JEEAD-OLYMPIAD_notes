## How the boundary was drawn

The split follows **syllabus relevance and usefulness**, not difficulty: Part I holds every standard JEE Main/Advanced heading of this module (the source chapter's plan parts 16–19), including hard core applications (mirrors, drifts, magnetic pressure, hysteresis, gapped circuits). Part II holds content whose *derivation or full treatment* sits outside the JEE syllabus or belongs to another paper's tradition — relativistic origin of magnetism, Helmholtz design algebra, demagnetising factors, pinch/cosine-theta engineering, Fermi acceleration, radiation-belt clock hierarchy, two-carrier transport, and order-of-magnitude apparatus estimates. Nothing is excluded merely for being hard. Topics are not claimed to "never appear in JEE"; extension material is derivable with the supplied information or belongs to another paper's syllabus.

## Deliberately not in these sheets

- Induction, inductance and AC (the next module); the B²/2μ₀ energy density is used, not derived from circuit work.
- Displacement current and radiation (electromagnetic-waves module).
- Quantum origin of spin, g = 2, exchange and superconductivity: named with their scales, derived nowhere here.
- The worked-exercise corpus of the source (C1–C14, E1–E20, Q1–Q60, OL1–OL12, the 36-question paper): the sheets compress the theory, methods and traps; the source chapter keeps the exercises.
