PRINTING — MAGNETISM + EMI + AC SHORT NOTES
================================

Paper and page setup
- A4 portrait (210 x 297 mm), one sheet per page.
- Use "Fit" / "Fit to printable area" — NOT "Fill", "Shrink to fit" or any crop mode.
- Margins: the artwork already keeps about a 10 mm safe inset; ordinary printer margins are fine.
- Colour recommended: navy body, teal headings, yellow result bands and red warnings all carry meaning.

Which file to print
- magnetism-short-notes.pdf — all 77 sheets in reading order (JEE first, then extensions).
- magnetism-jee.pdf — the 58 JEE Main + Advanced sheets only.
- magnetism-olympiad.pdf — the 19 specialised-extension sheets only.
- The PDFs embed each sheet as one lossless 2480 x 3508 px image at 300 DPI: what prints is exactly the
  delivered PNG, with no resampling or font substitution.

PNGs
- 2480 x 3508 px portrait, 300 DPI metadata (about 21 x 29.7 cm at print).
- Individual sheets are named JEE-01-lorentz.png ... JEE-58-rectifiers.png and Olympiad-01-relativity.png ... Olympiad-19-emi-estimates.png.

Reading order
- Part I (JEE) is complete in itself. Part II extends it; the boundary is syllabus relevance and
  usefulness, not difficulty. Complete Part I before Part II.
