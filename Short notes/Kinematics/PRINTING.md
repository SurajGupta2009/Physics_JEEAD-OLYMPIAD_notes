# Kinematics — printing

Use `kinematics-short-notes.pdf` for the full set: 26 JEE sheets first, followed by 10 Olympiad/specialised extensions. It is an A4-portrait PDF with exactly one PNG sheet per page and ordered bookmarks.

- Paper: **A4 portrait**; print **one sheet per page**.
- Scaling: **Fit to page** (or Fit printable area). **Do not use Fill or Crop.** Avoid poster tiling, multiple-up and borderless expansion.
- Each PNG: **2480 × 3508 px**, embedded **300 DPI**. The clear edge inset is at least 124 px (about 10.5 mm at 300 DPI).
- Main prose is 11.52 pt equivalent and explanations are 11.04 pt. Equations are typeset separately; do not downscale the page to force-fit it.
- Colour is preferred. Grayscale should retain contrast, but verify the yellow result panels on your printer.
- The combined ZIP contains PNGs only, in reading order with JEE first. Independent JEE and Olympiad numbering is intentional and visible on every sheet.

Test one page before printing the full set. The combined PDF is the easiest print route; the three PDFs and three PNG-only ZIPs are available in this folder. For archive contents, use the standalone ZIPs rather than extracting the PDFs.
