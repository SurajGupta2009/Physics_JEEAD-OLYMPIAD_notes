ELECTROSTATICS — A4 PRINTING

Prefer electrostatics-short-notes.pdf: 29 JEE pages, then 8 extensions.
Choose A4 PORTRAIT, ONE sheet per page, Fit to page / Fit printable area.
Do NOT choose Fill, Crop, borderless expansion, poster or multiple-up.
PNG originals are 2480 x 3508 pixels with 300 DPI metadata.
White safe inset is about 10.6 mm (125 px coordinates).
Body prose: 11.52 pt. Explanations/warnings: 11.04 pt. No body shrinking.
PDFs embed complete original indexed pixels and palettes losslessly.
Keep JEE- and Olympiad- filename prefixes when extracting PNG ZIPs.
Test a page first; colour preserves teal explanations, yellow results and
red traps. Check contrast if printing in grayscale.
The ZIPs contain PNGs ONLY. Clone/download the chapter folder for its
licensed font, gallery, editable content, build tools and source audit.
