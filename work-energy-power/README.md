# Work, Energy & Power — first principles to Olympiad

> [!note] Part 6 of [plan.md](../plan.md) · text-only Markdown chapter · written for Obsidian reading mode

**Scope.** Work by constant and variable forces; work of standard forces (gravity, friction, normal, tension, spring); the work–energy theorem (derived from $F=ma$); power; conservative forces and the closed-loop test; potential energy ($mgh$, $\frac{1}{2}kx^2$, $-GMm/r$); mechanical energy conservation (precise conditions); energy diagrams ($U(x)$, turning points, stability via $U''$); the energy/force decision rule; systems with internal work; non-conservative bookkeeping (friction, drag, thermal energy).

**Prerequisites.** PART 5 (Newton's laws, friction, constraint forces).

**The one idea.** Work is energy in transit; energy bookkeeping replaces force bookkeeping whenever path, not time, is the question.

**Contents.** Part 0 orientation; 1 intuition; 2 definitions; 3 core derivations (§3.1–3.14); 4 results ledger; 5 exemplars E1–E10; 6 archetypes and Q1–Q25; 7 toolkit; 8 traps; 9 playbook; 10 Olympiad block OL1–OL10; 11 paper; 12 marking; 13 formula sheet; 14 checkpoint.

**Coverage (Cengage floor).** *Cengage Mechanics II*, ch 2 §2.24–2.26 (rotational work and power, work–energy theorem, conservation of mechanical energy) + ch 1 §1.17–1.19 on impulse.

**Olympiad layer.** Block-on-wedge energy audit (the false result ignoring wedge KE); variable-mass energy audit (chain unrolling, leaking cart); CM-frame energy decomposition $K=\frac{1}{2}Mv_{\text{cm}}^2+K_{\text{rel}}$; effective potential for a bead on a rotating hoop and central-field orbits ($L^2/2mr^2$ barrier); satellite launch energy ($\sqrt{2}$ ratio); bouncing ball with restitution ($e$-dependent total distance/time); rolling-body energy; power-law force turning points.

**Hand-off.** The work–energy theorem and potential-energy diagrams are prerequisites for PART 7 (collisions), PART 8 (rotational dynamics), PART 9 (gravitational PE and orbits), and PART 10 (oscillations).
