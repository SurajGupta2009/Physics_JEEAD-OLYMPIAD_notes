# Nuclear Structure, Radioactivity, Fission & Fusion — first principles to Olympiad

> [!note] Part 26 of [plan.md](../plan.md) · text-only Markdown chapter · written for Obsidian reading mode

**Scope.** The nucleus as a saturated quantum liquid: sizes from $R\propto A^{1/3}$ and the
universal density of $2.3\times10^{17}$ kg/m$^3$; the mass defect and the binding-energy curve
that arbitrates every energy question; the liquid-drop mass formula and the valley of stability;
Q-values with the electron bookkeeping rule; alpha decay with the $(A-4)Q/A$ momentum split; beta
decay and the neutrino's continuous-spectrum argument; gamma de-excitation; the exponential decay
law with half-life and mean life; chains, secular and transient equilibrium; carbon, potassium
and isochron dating; fission, the chain reaction and reactor anatomy; fusion, the Coulomb barrier
and the honest (tunnelling) solar temperature; the p-p chain audit; and detectors with $\sqrt N$
counting statistics.

**Prerequisites.** PART 23 (energy bookkeeping, Poisson error logic, de Broglie wavelengths),
PART 24 (Bohr levels, muonic-atom size measurement, level-diagram literacy), PART 25
(de-excitation lines, electron-capture vacancies, Moseley's Z ordering the valley).

**The one idea.** Everything nuclear is energy accounting on a femtometre liquid drop: binding
sets what can happen, the decay constant sets when it happens, and tunnelling lets the Sun happen
at all.
