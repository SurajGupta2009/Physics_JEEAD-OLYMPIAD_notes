# X-rays, Moseley's Law, Bragg Diffraction & the Compton Effect — first principles to Olympiad

> [!note] Part 25 of [plan.md](../plan.md) · text-only Markdown chapter · written for Obsidian reading mode

**Scope.** Photon physics at keV energies: the Coolidge tube and its one-percent efficiency; the
continuous spectrum with the Duane-Hunt cutoff derived as the inverse photoelectric effect; the
characteristic spectrum and its threshold-voltage logic; Moseley's law derived from the screened
Bohr picture; exponential absorption, half-value thicknesses and imaging; Bragg's law with a
foolproof glancing-angle convention; lattice spacings and Avogadro's number from a crystal; the
Compton effect derived twice (scalar conservation and the four-momentum invariant) with recoil
energy and angle; the visible-light shift estimate; the pair-production threshold; and the
three-experiment synthesis that completes the photon.

**Prerequisites.** PART 23 (photon energy and momentum, diffraction logic, electron wavelengths)
and PART 24 (the Bohr ladder the characteristic lines ride on, Moseley as corollary).

**The one idea.** X-rays are photon physics with enough energy to see atoms and to knock electrons
free — and they prove that the photon carries momentum.

**Contents.** Part 0 orientation and coverage map keyed to the X-ray section names of Cengage ch 4;
1 intuition; 2 definitions and the angle conventions; 3 core derivations (§3.1-3.12); 4 results
ledger and concept checks; 5 exemplars E1-E12; 6 sixteen archetypes and Q1-Q28; 7 toolkit; 8 ten
traps; 9 playbook; 10 Olympiad block OL1-OL12 (Compton twice, the photon-cannot-vanish argument,
inverse Compton mirror, Duane-Hunt as h measurement, shielding constant, Avogadro with an error
budget, anode engineering, dose perspective, the sky as low-energy contrast, X-ray laser
obstacles, the relativistic wavelength correction); 11 the 36-question 200-mark paper; 12 marking
and audit; 13 formula sheet; 14 checkpoint and hand-off.

**Coverage (Cengage floor).** *Cengage Optics and Modern Physics* ch 4 Atomic Physics,
pp. 4.25-4.32: discovery, Coolidge tube, properties, applications, absorption, spectra and origin,
Moseley's law, solved examples, exercise bands — every heading has a row in the block-0 coverage
map. Bragg diffraction, powder rings, Avogadro-from-crystal, Compton (twice), pair production,
tube efficiency and anode heat are added by the sweep (INPhO/IPhO syllabus) and marked as such,
per plan.md §1.13's note that the X-ray half of ch 4 is shared and the coverage map must be keyed
