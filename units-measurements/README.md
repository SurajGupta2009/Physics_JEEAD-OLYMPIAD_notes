# Units, Dimensions & Measurement Errors — first principles to Olympiad

> [!note] Part 1 of [plan.md](../plan.md) · text-only Markdown chapter · written for Obsidian reading mode

**Scope.** SI base units and their history, dimensional formulae for 25+ quantities, the principle of homogeneity, dimensional derivation and analysis, dimensionless groups (Reynolds, Mach, Froude previewed), significant figures and rounding, error classification (systematic, random, gross), error propagation (sums, products, powers, quadrature), instruments (vernier caliper, screw gauge with zero-error handling), graph reading and linearisation, and the Fermi estimation method.

**Prerequisites.** None — this is the head of the sequence.

**The one idea.** Every measurement is a comparison plus an honest statement of how wrong it could be.

**Contents.** Part 0 orientation; 1 intuition; 2 definitions; 3 core derivations (§3.1–3.12); 4 results ledger; 5 exemplars E1–E10; 6 archetypes and Q1–Q25; 7 toolkit; 8 traps; 9 playbook; 10 Olympiad block OL1–OL10; 11 paper; 12 marking; 13 formula sheet; 14 checkpoint.

**Coverage (Cengage floor).** *Cengage Mechanics I*, ch 3 Units and Dimensions (SI system, dimensional formulae, homogeneity, limitations of dimensional analysis, significant figures, errors, instruments) with ch 1 Basic Mathematics folded in (derivatives, integrals, binomial approximation, maxima–minima).

**Olympiad layer.** Least-squares fitting (normal equations for a straight line), the Buckingham π theorem (applied to the pendulum and the blast-radius problem $R\propto(Et^2/\rho)^{1/5}$), the $g$-measurement design problem, the $\chi^2$ goodness-of-fit criterion, standard error of the mean and the $\sqrt{N}$ rule, Avogadro from an oil-film estimate.
