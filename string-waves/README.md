## What is in this topic

`String-waves.md` is the complete master text adhering to the repository's strict 8-part pedagogical architecture:
- **Part 1**: Foundations & Physical Motivation (transverse vs longitudinal, harmonic wave kinematics)
- **Part 2**: The Wave Equation & Tension Derivations (Newton's 2nd law for $dm$, d'Alembert solution, limits)
- **Part 3**: Particle Velocity, Acceleration & Wave Profile Mapping ($v_p = -v \partial y/\partial x$)
- **Part 4**: Energy Density, Power Transmission & Intensity ($u_k = u_p$, $\langle P \rangle = \frac{1}{2}\mu\omega^2 A^2 v$)
- **Part 5**: Boundary Reflections & Impedance Matching (fixed vs free end, transmission across density steps)
- **Part 6**: Superposition, Standing Waves & Normal Modes (nodes/antinodes, sonometer laws, Melde's experiment)
- **Part 7**: Advanced & Olympiad Extensions (heavy hanging rope $v(y)=\sqrt{gy}$, WKB approximation, non-uniform strings)
- **Part 8**: Playbook, Traps & Mental Decision Trees (sign conventions, energy paradoxes, fast estimation)
- **Part 9**: 36-Question Examination Paper (Single Correct, Multi-Correct, Numerical, Olympiad Long-Form)
- **Part 10**: Complete Step-by-Step Solutions with marking breakdown and limit verifications
- **Part 11**: Printable Formula Reference Sheet with conditions of validity
